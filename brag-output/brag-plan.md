# Brag Plan: ReplyMuse

## Planning Rubric (9 Questions)

1. **What is the app?**
   ReplyMuse is an AI review reply studio that writes authentic responses to Google reviews for local cafes, restaurants, and small businesses in seconds.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Turn customer feedback into repeat guests — every reply, in your voice, in seconds."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   Warm cream frosted-glass styling (`#F7F3E8`), rich forest green (`#1F7A5C`), vibrant coral CTA pill (`#EE6A4B`), paired with the live 5-star review-to-reply interactive card.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The real review moment from the landing page: Priya K.'s 5-star review of cardamom pistachio croissants transforming into a warm artisanal cafe reply, followed by the one-click copy interaction.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   18 seconds (4 tightly-paced, beat-aligned scenes).

6. **What tone fits best?**
   - **Preset**: `polished`
   - **Creative direction**: Warm editorial product film for craft businesses and modern founders.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Warm, uplifting rhythm bed (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`), clean soft UI clicks on cards and button interactions.

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Tired of robotic AI responses to customer reviews? We built ReplyMuse: paste a Google review, get an authentic reply in your unique brand voice in seconds."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Customer review arrives (5 stars) $\rightarrow$ Voice analysis matches cafe tone $\rightarrow$ Instant personalized reply drafted $\rightarrow$ One-click copy.

---

## Storyboard (18.0s Total)

- **Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s | Duration 3.5s)**
  - *Visual*: Dark forest green accent badge "CRAFTED FOR LOCAL BUSINESS OWNERS", bold editorial serif title: "Reply to every Google review in seconds."
  - *Motion*: Elegant spring-up reveal of title with wavy coral underline accent.
  - *SFX*: Soft whoosh / chime.

- **Scene 2: The Customer Review (3.5s – 7.5s | Duration 4.0s)**
  - *Visual*: Authentic Google review card from Priya K. (5 stars: "Their cardamom pistachio croissants are the best in Bangalore!").
  - *Motion*: Card slides in and settles with a soft shadow and 5 golden stars pop sequentially.
  - *SFX*: Subtle pop and paper tap.

- **Scene 3: The Voice-Matched Reply (7.5s – 13.5s | Duration 6.0s)**
  - *Visual*: "AI reply crafted in your voice" badge pulses in coral. Live typing effect of the artisanal reply: "Hi Priya, Thanks so much! Our bakers pour their hearts into those croissants... see you this weekend!"
  - *Action*: Green badge highlights "Artisanal Café Voice", Copy button pulses with checkmark "Copied!".
  - *SFX*: Light mechanical keyboard ticks, subtle pleasant click on copy.

- **Scene 4: The Outro & CTA (13.5s – 18.0s | Duration 4.5s)**
  - *Visual*: ReplyMuse logo and brand mark, tagline "EVERY REPLY, IN YOUR VOICE", coral pill button: "Get started free — replymuse.vercel.app".
  - *Motion*: Clean scale-in with soft glow and subtle music fade-out.
  - *SFX*: Warm closing chime.
